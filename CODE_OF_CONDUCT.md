# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome contributors of different
backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews,
discussions, and public events where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Include links or screenshots when
safe to do so. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Enforcement will
consider context, impact, repetition, and the safety of affected people. Good-
faith correction and accountability are encouraged; retaliation is prohibited.
